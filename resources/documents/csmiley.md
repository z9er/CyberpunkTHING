**What can Limited Hud do for me, how do I use the Limited HUD global toggles, and how is it setup for each experience by default?**



Limited HUD (LHUD) allows for HUD widgets to be shown/hidden on certain events, reducing visual clutter while keeping relevant information visible.



Default key bindings are F8 for the global hotkey, and F6 for the minimap hotkey. Key bindings and HUD visibility criteria can be modified in game from Mods > Unified Mod Settings > LHUD. Additional HUD options such as scan/ricochet object coloring and hiding interaction prompts are located in LHUD Addons



While both editions of WTNC include dynamic widgets, THING offers a more minimal HUD.





**What does Trigger Mode Control do for me?**



This is a framework that allows certain weapons to swap firemodes at the press of a keybind. Default binding is T, but this can be changed from in-game mod settings





**What does Berserk Unchained do for me?**



All Berserk implants will allow ranged weapons / gadgets / inhalers, and can be deactivated / reactivated to retain unspent charge





**How does Alternative Berserk change the Berserk functionality?**



With this mod present, absolute invulnerability and immortality are removed from Berserk.

In exchange, health regeneration is preserved (and enhanced) during the effect and a new stacking buff allows for extra damage resistance to be accumulated through kills; allowing for unparalleled durability (in synergy with base tier Body perks) while retaining some degree of risk.





**How can Body Shield save my life?**



While carrying a body, V is given a flat amount of armor that provides an extra layer of protection. This can be very impactful in the early game, but tapers off in practicality as enemies become more lethal.





**What can Buttslinger Quickmelee do for me?**



Quickmelee attacks with ranged weapons will stun enemies, and open them up to grabs and finishers for a brief period. 





**What are all these chips and how do they work?**



These are part of the Neuralware mods’ new chipware system, which allows these chips to be installed into your neural processor located next to your face cyberware.



Reflex chips grant activatable abilities in the form of Techniques, which are powerful maneuvers or buffs that typically cost Neural Capacity to activate.



Memory chips cost Cyberware Capacity to equip. They grant Perks (without actually unlocking them in the perk tree) and unique passive abilities that grow stronger with the chip's Attunement level. While slotted, gaining experience in the related Skill increases Attunement, with higher tier Neural Processors, accelerating progress. 

Some Memory Chips have hidden abilities that can only be uncovered after fulfilling special requirements, related to the theme of that chip.



Software chips require RAM to run. They provide a variety of different bonuses or abilities, and require a valid License to operate, which typically expires after a number of uses and requires payment to renew. Cracking a chip bypasses the License requirement.





**Why is overclock killing me in THING, and how does Overclock Overheat work?**



Overclock can now be portioned by interrupting and re-activating the effect, but if you run completely out of charge then you will start burning.





**Why does it feel like my recoil is higher in THING?**



By default, draw / holster speed in THING is 60% slower than in vanilla, recoil is 10% stronger, and recoil recovery is disabled. This can be modified in-game from Mods > Unified Mod Settings > Weapon Handling Control





**How can I use Enhanced Craft to make cool looking guns?**



Crafting Specs no longer have randomized skins, skin variants can be chosen with C and Z in the crafting menu





**Why is my crosshair missing in THING?**



All melee and ranged weapons except smart / cyberweapons have their crosshair off in most situations. Opacities and overrides can be modified from the Crosshair Fade section of the mod settings





**When can I start using the Kiroshi Optics Night Vision mod and how does it work?**



Starting at tier 3 (blue) cyberware, all Kiroshi implants have access to night vision, which is toggled with F3





**How does Metro Pocket Guide help me?**



MPG finds the shortest route between any two metro stations. From the map, select Route near the bottom of the screen, choose your start and end stations, press Confirm, enter the subway, and choose the tracked route.
