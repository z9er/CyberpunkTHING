**What can Limited Hud do for me, how do I use the Limited HUD global toggles, and how is it setup for each experience by default?**



Limited HUD (LHUD) allows for HUD widgets to be shown/hidden on certain events, reducing visual clutter while keeping relevant information visible.



Default key bindings are F8 for the global hotkey, and F6 for the minimap hotkey. Key bindings and HUD visibility criteria can be modified in game from Mods > Unified Mod Settings > LHUD. Additional HUD options such as scan/ricochet object coloring and hiding interaction prompts are located in LHUD Addons



The collection makes the following modifications to the HUD:

Overall:

&#x09;Action buttons: with weapon, in combat, stealth, or in vehicle

&#x09;Crouch indicator: in stealth

&#x09;Weapon roster: with weapon, in combat, or in stealth

&#x09;Stamina: visible in combat, with weapon, or when stamina is not full

&#x09;World markers:

&#x09;	Quest: always on

&#x09;	Loot: always on

&#x09;	POI: visible in vehicle, with scanner, with zoom, or when tracked

&#x09;	Owned vehicles: visible with scanner or with zoom

&#x09;	Device interactions: visible with scanner

WTNC-specific: 

&#x09;Hotkey hints: always on

&#x09;Minimap: always on

&#x09;Quest tracker: visible out of combat, in vehicle, with zoom, in danger zones, or during journal updates

&#x09;Player healthbar: visible when health/memory is not full, with buffs/quickhacks, in combat, in stealth, or with weapon

&#x09;World markers:

&#x09;	Combat: always on

THING-specific:

&#x09;Hotkey hints: in vehicle, or with weapon

&#x09;Minimap: in vehicle, with zoom, or when wanted

&#x09;Quest tracker: with zoom, or during journal updates (5sec)

&#x09;Player healthbar: visible when health/memory is not full, with quickhacks, in combat, in stealth, or with weapon

&#x09;World markers:

&#x09;	Combat: out of combat, in vehicle, or with scanner
