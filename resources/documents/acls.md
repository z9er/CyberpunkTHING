# Combat AI Ideas: TLDR

## Behaviors

### Netrunner Hack Combos & Coordination
- **Hack lands → team capitalizes:** weapon glitch lands → melee pushes; reboot optics lands → snipers and squishies reposition *(Q, Tyber, Atrakin)*
- **Lockdown combo:** netrunner hits cripple movement → flashbang → smoke on you → melee flanks *(b)*
- **Player out of cover →** cripple movement + flashbangs *(b)*
- **Sandevistan / aggressive player in the open →** cyberware malfunction or cripple movement to strand you *(Atrakin)*
- **Reboot optics before a push or grenade** so you don't see it coming *(Atrakin)*
- **Reboot optics while a tech sniper lines up a shot through a wall** *(b)*
- **While a netrunner uploads,** the others suppress you; melee rushes to make a 3-layer wall: melee > suppressors > hacker *(Jv)*
- **Heavies body-block for the netrunner** during an upload; if there are no heavies, the closest enemies hold ground *(Van1shGold)*
- **Ally at low health + netrunner present →** weapon glitch or malfunction on you to cover the ally *(MishaD)*
- **Sniper + netrunner up →** glitch, cripple, or optics, then Call Backup *(MishaD)*
- **Smoke/flash + Ping;** if you're already pinged, go straight to malfunction hacks *(MishaD)*
- **Each netrunner specializes in one hack** (NR1 synapse burnout, NR2 weapon glitch, and so on) *(Atrakin)*
- **Ghost/bait enemy** to lure the player *(MishaD)*
- **Netrunner hacks a car and drives it over you** (Delamain-style) *(b, likely impossible)*
- **You're hiding under chain-supported boxes →** netrunner drops them on you *(b, likely impossible)*

### Counterplay (in-fight reactions to what you keep doing)
- **Trigger:** you keep killing them the same way during a fight. The threshold is set by the highest-ranking enemy, so killing elites first delays it *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Tech weapons →** cover matters less to them; they rush or flush you *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Smart weapons →** heavy cover use, short bursts to cut lock-on time, netrunners go for cyberware malfunction *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Melee →** they keep their distance, cover optional, netrunners go for cripple movement *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Explosives →** they close distance while spreading out and avoid red hazards *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Stealth (2+ bodies found) →** buddy system, no easy solo picks, guards hold on the netrunner while it traces *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Optical camo →** they prioritize grenades and spray fire and buddy up *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Netrunning →** 1–2 guards stay on their netrunner, the rest rush you *(Gerber E-cigs, Van1shGold)*
- **Sandevistan →** cyberware malfunction and EMP nades become top priority; their own sandy users try to anchor you *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Heavy weapons →** high cover use and patience; enemies you aren't pressuring return fire, and your reloads get punished hard *(Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Blade deflect →** an enemy who gets a bullet deflected back stops firing until your block is off them or dropped *(Gerber E-cigs)*

### Tech Weapons / Shooting Through Cover
- **You camp cover too long →** netrunner pings you → tech gunners fire through the wall *(Atrakin, cal)*
- **Netrunner upload marks you:** regular enemies push, tech gunners shoot through walls *(cal)*
- **Eye cyberware lets an enemy briefly mark you** *(cal)*
- **More aggressive blindfire** from tech users at the cover you're behind *(cal)*
- **Smart weapons track better when you're marked,** with limited tracking around corners *(cal)*
- **Tech users stay in cover and use charged shots** instead of running into the open *(E)*
- **Tech users aim first, reload behind cover,** and others push in pairs or more *(MishaD)*
- **You're using a wall-pen weapon →** enemies rush more, since cover matters less *(Jv)*

### Grenades, Smokes, EMP
- **Shoot your grenades mid-air,** especially easy lobs *(Blast)*
- **Shoot or hack your grenades if you hold them too long** *(E)*
- **Raise the cap of 2 enemy grenades in the air at once** *(dnbnhlp)*
- **You use active cyberware →** they respond with an EMP *(DuskAtDawn)*
- **They realize they're being sniped →** smoke at their feet, run for indoors *(DuskAtDawn)*
- **Long-range opener or alert →** pop smokes (Militech, scavs) *(Van1shGold)*
- **Smokes wipe pings and tags** *(b)*
- **Poison grenades for area denial** to force you to move *(E)*
- **Non-explosive grenades when defending their own base** *(Jv)*

### Thrown Knives
- **Melee enemies throw knives when you back away,** or when you're shooting at close range. A hit causes a short stagger or stun; poisoned knives can block sprinting or slow you *(touki)*

### Environmental Hazards
- **You're near a hazard (barrel, canister, fuse box) →** they shoot or hack it *(Blast, DuskAtDawn, E, cal)*

### Positioning, Cover, Fatal Funnel
- **Fatal funnel fix:** stop enemies streaming one by one up stairs into your crosshair *(Drazgul, Jv)*
- **Nade the chokepoint** instead of walking into it *(Do The Roar)*
- **Allies died within ~2m of each other →** avoid that spot, take cover, nade it *(Tyber)*
- **Hold angles and chokepoints,** especially stairs; armored enemies creep up without overexposing *(Jv)*
- **Don't clump up;** attack as several small groups from different angles *(DuskAtDawn, TMYK)*
- **Getting picked off one by one →** regroup for safety *(Do The Roar)*
- **Leg-cyberware enemies use jumps to flank,** gain angles, get behind you, shoot mid-air *(cal)*
- **Snipers move to high ground before firing** *(DuskAtDawn)*
- **Snipers take their shot faster** *(E)*
- **Combat start:** ranged enemies with mobility (super jump) reposition to aim; close-range enemies open with a strong attack to buy time *(MishaD)*
- **Get in a nearby vehicle and run you over** *(DuskAtDawn)*
- **You're knocked down or run over →** they rush in to finish you while you're down *(TMYK)*

### Unreachable Player / Reinforcements
- **You're somewhere they can't reach →** break line of sight, hide, call reinforcements that can handle it (air support, teleport, remote hacks), send drones to flank *(Reondryt, b)*
- **Allies dropping and no one can trace you →** call reinforcements who can *(Reondryt)*

### CQC
- **Strafe, side-dodge, slide around** (ENC-style), vary standing and crouching; RDR2-style, paired with time dilation *(b, Q)*
- **Melee rushes while strafing;** ranged backs off to cover or the nearest melee ally, or switches to melee *(Atrakin)*
- **Run-and-gun with low accuracy** as self cover fire *(Atrakin)*
- **Knock down or disable you,** or go max aggression, depending on enemy type *(Do The Roar)*
- **You get in close on a gunner with melee →** he gun-butts you to break your rhythm *(b)*
- **Katana users deflect bullets more; mantis blade users focus on fast attacks** *(Van1shGold, b)*. Unclear whether enemies can deflect at all.

### Sandevistan Enemies
- **General:** use sandies to strafe and reposition (still hittable) instead of matrix-dodging in the open *(b, Gerber E-cigs)*
- **Sandy sniper volley:** sandy activation, then 3 rapid sniper shots in time dilation, on a cooldown *(b)*
- **Sandy sniper:** after a few shots or losing line of sight, reposition fast with sandy + double jump *(Atrakin)*
- **Sandy AR:** back off and find cover when you get close, hop cover to cover, reload or lob nades during sandy *(Atrakin)*
- **Sandy shotgun:** close in, fire, reload; use sandy to stay on you if you run or while they reload; break for cover when hurt or the sandy's nearly out *(Atrakin, Van1shGold)*
- **Sandy melee:** close distance and get aggressively in your face *(Atrakin, Van1shGold)*
- **Defensive sandy (shotgun/LMG):** dash between you and the sniper or netrunner they're protecting *(Van1shGold)*

### Weapon Switching
- **No shotguns at sniper range;** swap to handguns or ARs, elites to snipers *(DuskAtDawn)*

### Stealth / Awareness
- **4 awareness states** *(Atrakin)*:
  - **Calm:** normal routines
  - **Suspicious:** individual investigates odd stuff, then returns to Calm
  - **Certain:** body found, gunshot, or repeated sightings; alerts the group, everyone stays on edge
  - **Alert:** permanent; searches your last known spot, then fans out
- **Finding a body never fades back to calm** (maybe Maelstrom excepted) *(Reondryt, Atrakin)*
- **Silenced shots still trigger Certain at short range,** ~6m *(Atrakin, E)*
- **Buddy shot in front of them →** they know the direction it came from *(Do The Roar, Atrakin)*
- **They investigate where you shot from,** not where the bullet hit *(Reondryt)*
- **Repeated distraction hacks raise suspicion** *(Reondryt)*:
  - Netrunners have a lower threshold; brutes may just ignore it
  - Escalates to a trace, alerting allies, or calling a techie
- **Jammer blocks their radar →** owner warns nearby allies out loud, more people search, they try to trace *(Reondryt)*
- **Subnet awareness:** stealth takedown → other netrunners get suspicious after a short delay; quickhacks on devices alert netrunners more *(cal, Do The Roar)*
- **Animals go suspicious →** sprint around searching, fast but less thorough *(cal)*
- **Corpos search high spots and likely hiding places,** use recon and tracking hacks, and go into lockdown when a body turns up *(cal)*

### Faction Behavior Flavor
- **Maelstrom:** reckless rush, bad cover use, shotguns and explosives, no smokes or flashes, mantis/gorilla rushers *(cal, E, Jv)*
- **Arasaka:** hold ground, wait for reinforcements *(Jv)*
- **Corpos (general):** professional flanking and maneuvering, constant pressure, faster reinforcements *(cal)*
- **Militech:** organized formations to push and flank *(epinephrin3)*. Lower threshold for calling heavies, and grenades everywhere *(Tyber)*:
  - **You're in cover →** flash + frag or incendiary to flush you out, then the squad rushes
  - **You're in the open →** AoE nades to pin you down
  - **Alerted with no eyes on you →** spam recon grenades
  - **Alone in combat →** drop smoke and regroup
- **Scavs:** conservative; only push when you're debuffed or reloading, otherwise sit in cover spamming EMP/flash/smoke *(cal)*
- **Tyger Claws:** fodder aggressive, bosses hang back taking potshots, push back hard when pressured then escape; more reinforcement calls. Halo-style panic when an elite dies *(cal)*
- **VDB:** netrunners hang back camo'd and debuff anyone who pushes; stack hacks together instead of taking turns; ping and mark you if they can't see you *(cal)*
- **Animals:** melee use gapclosers more often and from farther, jump up to you if you're high, better flanking; coordinate with flash and smoke toward the last heard shot *(cal, E)*
- **Leader killed →** lower ranks panic and get reckless (Halo Elites) *(Tyber)*

### Design Notes
- **Counter-behaviors need a tell first.** Enemies shouldn't know your build ahead of time; a shot or two should reveal it (deck, sandy, tech weapon), then they switch from default to counter *(Atrakin, b)*
- **Keep counters soft, not hard:** annoying enough to make you adapt, never a shutdown *(b)*
- **Counters shouldn't erase faction identity,** e.g. no surprise netrunners on Animals *(Atrakin)*
- **Advanced tactics corpo-only?** Suggested by Atrakin; b prefers a baseline of competence for every faction, since most fights aren't against corpos

- **Netrunners spawn drones.** Already in Cyber Enemies.
- **Enemies use ricochet.** No ricochet mechanic exists.
- **Netrunners trigger NCPD city defenses.** Unlikely to work.
- **Gangs with police contacts drop a wanted level.** Already happens via casualties.
- **Drug buffs when combat breaks.** Already in Cyber Enemies.
